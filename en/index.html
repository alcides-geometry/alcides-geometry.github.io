Research

Geometry, curvature
and rigidity.

I study Differential Geometry, with an emphasis on submanifold theory and Geometric Analysis. My main interests are rigidity problems for submanifolds in space forms — when an isometric immersion is unique up to rigid motions — and the study of (m)-quasi-Einstein manifolds and stable hypersurfaces. I explore connections with geometric partial differential equations and prescribed curvature.

Research interests

  • Differential Geometry
  • Submanifold Theory
  • Geometric Analysis
  • Submanifold Rigidity
  • (m)-quasi-Einstein Manifolds
  • Stable Hypersurfaces
