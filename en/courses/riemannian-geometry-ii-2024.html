Riemannian Geometry II

Aug. – Nov. 2024

Problem sets

Problem set 01 ↗
Syllabus

Spectral geometry and elliptic operators; bundles and Hodge theory; Bochner and Weitzenböck identities; isometric immersions and area variations; minimal and constant mean curvature submanifolds; Reilly formulas and applications.

Reading list
  • Marcos Dajczer e Ruy Tojeiro — Submanifold Theory. Springer.
  • Antonio Caminha — Tópicos de Geometria Diferencial. SBM.
  • Shigeyuki Morita — Geometry of Differential Forms. AMS.
