SAGA

SAGA is the Seminar on Geometric Analysis and Applications at UFPE’s Department of Mathematics. It brings together researchers, graduate students and undergraduate research students to discuss current topics in Differential Geometry, Geometric Analysis and related fields. Talks are open to the entire academic community.

Comparing Neumann and Dirichlet Spectra via the Buckling Problem

Vinícius Guardiano Souza

Institute of Mathematics · Federal University of Alagoas (UFAL)

In this talk, we will present an approach to comparing Neumann and Dirichlet spectra on bounded domains in Riemannian manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature and boundaries with nonnegative mean curvature. The central idea is to introduce the buckling problem as an intermediate spectral problem between the two.
